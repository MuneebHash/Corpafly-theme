# Direction C: Order Desk

**The idea.** Most Corpafly buyers arrive knowing what they need, so the homepage is an order desk, not a brochure. Pick a type, set colors and pack counts, watch the price per lanyard update, and check out from the first screen.

**What is glass**
- The header, which carries a running cart total.
- The receipt floating over the live preview. It is the main glass moment: the hanging straps blur through it.
- The phone summary bar (total, price per lanyard, Add), the cart drawer and the finder's suggested order.

Everything you fill in sits on solid white sheets.

**What moves**
- Prices roll digit by digit (240 ms).
- A lanyard slides onto its peg when a color is added and lifts off when removed (320 ms). Receipt lines drop into their slots.
- The finder's answer rises into place, and the break-away clasp springs open when you pull the strap.
- On load, only the desk rises (480 ms). Under reduced motion, values change instantly and the pull demo becomes a Before / After toggle.

**Why it fits Corpafly buyers**
Office managers reorder the same thing, so Buy again and the order link make it one step. Teachers and event teams color-code, so mixed colors share one pack size, with the price per lanyard always in view. Travelers want one small pack: the Travel row loads it in one tap. Quantities and prices are set in Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, so nobody misreads a number.
