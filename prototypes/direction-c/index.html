    Break-away, in plain words.

    The Break-Away Lanyard has a safety clasp at the neck. If the strap is caught or pulled, the clasp opens and the lanyard comes off.

    Choose it for kids and schools, hospitals and warehouses, or anywhere a caught strap is a risk.
