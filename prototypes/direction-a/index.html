Break-away

Pull it and it lets go.

The Break-Away Lanyard has a safety clasp at the neck. When the strap is pulled, the clasp opens and the lanyard comes away.

  1. Where

    The clasp sits in the strap, at the neck.

  2. When it opens

    A pull on the strap or the badge opens the clasp, so the lanyard lets go.
