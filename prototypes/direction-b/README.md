# Direction B: Diamond Grid

**Idea.** The Corpafly mark is built from 45-degree strokes on a diamond grid, so the store is drawn on that grid too: spec plates with chevron-cut corners, hairlines and mono numerals over a fine lattice. It reads like a datasheet: exact and calm.

**Glass.** Five places only: the header (dark over dark sections, light over light ones), the hero lens, the order bar once it docks, the "Your plan" card and the cart drawer. The lens is baked: the lattice inside it is redrawn magnified under a plain blur.

**Motion.** Once on load, in 1.1 s, the mark's six strokes slide in along their own axes, then a blue strap draws itself through the gap between the C and the F and its J-hook drops in. Plates wipe in on a 45-degree edge, spec rows take a 45-degree highlight on hover, and a chosen color tile settles with a small spring. Reduced motion shows the final state.

**Why it fits.** Office managers, schools and event teams buy on facts. Each plate puts pack, colors, price and price per lanyard in one table, and the order bar and planner turn needs into packs fast.

**Brand note.** The hero uses the mark's geometry as a cropped background graphic in a dark key (deep teal C, black F). This brand device needs Muneeb's approval; the header logo keeps its approved colors.

**Fonts.** Trimmed OFL cuts of Archivo and Martian Mono, licences in `fonts/`.
