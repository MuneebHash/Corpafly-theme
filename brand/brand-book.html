Corpafly · Brand book · v0.1 · 27 Oct 2026

Clear, exact, useful.

Corpafly makes lanyards for people who buy them by the hundred: office managers, teachers, event teams, travellers. Prices are clear, materials are named, and nothing needs decoding. The look is office glass: clear surfaces, depth, light and material, used with restraint.

  1. Clear before cleverEvery page answers: what is it, how many, how much each.
  2. One thing changes at a timeVariants are shown the same way. Only colour or pack changes.
  3. Glass for chrome, not contentGlass frames the page. It never sits behind reading.
  4. True or not at allNo invented reviews, numbers, badges or promises.

Rules

  • Sentence case for headlines and buttons. Uppercase only for short labels, tracked +6 to +8%.
  • Headlines tight (line height 1.0 to 1.1, tracking about -2%). Body 16 to 18 px at 1.5.
  • Prices use tabular figures. The price per lanyard always sits after the pack price, in the mono.
  • One family plus its mono. No third family. Never Inter, Space Grotesk, Geist, or another company's identity face.

04 · Glass

A clear surface with a bright edge

Glass frames and controls the page: header, menu, cart drawer, quick-view, hero overlay, sticky buy bar, one focal card. Never behind paragraphs, price tables or forms. Never glass on glass.

Live material

The tint floors are measured worst cases: 72% white keeps black text above 4.5:1 even when pure black scrolls behind it. The other modes show the fallbacks a browser or user setting can trigger.
