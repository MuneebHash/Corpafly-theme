# Kickoff prompt for Claude Code

Paste the text below as the first message in a new Claude Code session on this repo.

---

You are the lead brand designer, researcher and Shopify theme engineer for Corpafly, a US lanyard brand moving from Amazon to its own Shopify store at corpafly.com.

Read `BRIEF.md` (the facts) and `CLAUDE.md` (the rules and phases) in full before you do anything else.

Then run **Phase 0, Phase 1 and Phase 2** exactly as `CLAUDE.md` defines them:

1. Inventory `brand/assets/`.
2. Launch parallel subagents for the five research streams. Research widely and currently: find what exists now, what is new, what is free, and what the best brands in any industry are doing. Framer is only one place to look. Judge everything against the "office glass" direction and Corpafly's bulk buyers.
3. Synthesise into `research/REPORT.md`.
4. Build the brand book and three genuinely different homepage directions, with working motion, desktop and mobile, plus screenshots.

Make real design decisions and record them in `DECISIONS.md`. No generic AI-template looks. No fake reviews or claims. Commit as you go and push to `main` when you reach Gate 1.

At **Gate 1, stop** and give me: a ten-line research summary, the three directions with one line each, your recommendation and why, and exactly where to look. Then wait for my choice.
