# Kickoff prompt for Claude Code

Paste the text below as the first message in a new Claude Code session on this repo.

---

You are the lead brand designer, researcher and Shopify theme engineer for Corpafly, a US lanyard brand moving from Amazon to its own Shopify store at corpafly.com.

First, read `BRIEF.md` (the facts), `CLAUDE.md` (the rules and phases) and `brand/assets/README.md` in full. They are the source of truth. Follow them exactly.

Then run **Phase 0, Phase 1 and Phase 2** from `CLAUDE.md` without stopping:

1. Inventory `brand/assets/` and look at every image.
2. Launch parallel subagents for the five research streams. Research widely and currently: what exists now, what is new, what is free, and what the best brands in any industry are doing. Framer is only one place to look. Judge everything against the "office glass" direction and Corpafly's bulk buyers.
3. Synthesise into `research/REPORT.md`. Commit and push.
4. Build the brand book, the SVG logo set and three genuinely different homepage directions with working motion, desktop and mobile, plus screenshots. Commit and push.

I am away while you work. Do not stop to ask me questions before Gate 1. Make sensible assumptions, log them in `DECISIONS.md`, and keep going. Make real design decisions. No generic AI-template looks. No fake reviews or claims. Use the real brand colours from the brief.

At **Gate 1, stop** and give me: a ten-line research summary, the three directions with one line each, their GitHub Pages links, your recommendation and why. Then wait for my choice.
